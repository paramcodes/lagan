Problem set 6 — graph traversal and shortest paths

DueThu 19 Mar, 23:59 Worth35 points · 6% of the course Late work−5% a day, up to 3 days AttemptsUnlimited until the deadline

What to do

Answer all five sections. Sections 1 to 3 are short and worth 4 points each. Sections 4 and 5 carry the rest of the marks and expect a written argument, not just a number.

Where the marks go Half of every section is the answer, half is the justification. An answer with no stated invariant or complexity bound scores at most half, even when it is right.

Sections

  • 1 — Representation. Build an adjacency list for the graph in figures/network.txt and report the memory cost against an adjacency matrix for the same graph.
  • 2 — Traversal. Give the breadth-first order from node H. Show the queue at each step.
  • 3 — Depth-first. Give the discovery and finish times, and classify every edge as tree, back, forward, or cross.
  • 4 — Shortest paths, unweighted. Prove that breadth-first search returns a shortest path, and state the assumption your proof needs.
  • 5 — Weighted graphs. Dijkstra fails on one graph in figures/negative.txt. Name the edge that breaks it and explain what the algorithm returns instead.

Format

One PDF, at most six pages, with your student number in the header and no name. Diagrams may be hand-drawn and photographed. Code listings are not required, but if you include them they must run.

The Lagan system

One palette, one typeface, three shapes, and two devices that carry meaning instead of decoration. Everything on the other screens is built from what is on this page.

Palette

Ink is the institution. Chalk yellow always means “you are here”.
Ink#0C0D0CRail, headings, the one dark hero. Never used for body text on light.
Ink 2#171817Hover and active states inside the rail.
Canvas#F4F4F2Page background. A working grey, not cream.
Surface#FFFFFFPanels, tables, anything that sits above the canvas.
Line#E0E1DEPanel edges and table rules. Decoration, and exempt from the contrast floor.
Edge#7C7E77Boundaries of operable things only: inputs, chips, outline buttons, the drop zone. 3.4:1 on white, because a control's edge is how you find it.
Quiet text#616360 · #6E706CLabels, captions, chart axes. The second step was darkened from #8C8E8B so both clear 4.5:1 on the canvas.
Brand#111311Primary actions, links, on-pace progress. Black, not a colour. 18.8:1 on white.
Brand soft#E9EAE7Course codes, completed markers, quiet confirmations.
Chalk#F2E679The only loud colour. Marks position: today, the current module, the hero action.
Due soft#FBEFD6 / #8A5200Something is due soon. Text on the soft fill, never the fill alone.
Alert soft#FAE7E4 / #A62B22Overdue, missing, or broken. Reserved for time, not for errors in the abstract.

Rules

The reasons this looks like itself instead of like every other dashboard.

Do

+Lead a page with the decision or the deadline. “Two things due before Friday” beats “Welcome back, Rovan”.
+Let the deadline set the colour. Amber means soon, red means late, and nothing else may use those two.
+Reach for a hairline rule before a card. Containers cost attention; every one has to earn it.
+Keep one loud element per screen — here it is the dark hero with the chalk button.
+Write the empty and error states as directions: what happened, what to do, where to go.

Don’t

−No grid of identical rounded cards as the home page. Courses are a ledger; they read faster as rows.
−No tracked-out capitals above every heading, and no middot-separated meta strings used as decoration.
−No percentage without its comparison. 52% alone tells the student nothing.
−No second accent colour, and no tint of the brand invented on the spot. If it is not a token, it is not a colour.
−No animation that only decorates. One draw-in on load, and only motion that answers a tap.

Access and motion

+Body text at 15px with a 1.6 line height; nothing below 12px, ever.
+Every interactive target is at least 32px, 44px on the touch layouts.
+Status is never colour alone: every pill carries its own words.
+Text clears 4.5:1 and control boundaries clear 3:1, measured, not eyeballed. That is why there are two line tokens: --line for decoration, --edge for the edge of anything you can operate.
+Focus is a 2px ink ring on every control, drawn with :focus-visible so the mouse never sees it. On the black rail and the hero the ring switches to chalk, because a black ring on black is not a focus indicator.
+One page-load transition, 340ms. prefers-reduced-motion turns it off completely.
+The distribution chart and the spine both carry a text alternative.
